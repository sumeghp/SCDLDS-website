Stochastic Calculus & Mathematical Finance

Course #: SCDLDS-301
Term: Fall 2025
Instructor: Sandeep Juneja
Completed Course

This course maps the complex mathematical foundation of continuous-time asset pricing. Initiating with measure-theoretic probability frameworks, students progress through standard Brownian Motion models, stochastic differential integrations, and pricing frameworks. Beyond applications in quant finance, mastering these continuous-time structures is becoming highly advantageous for understanding structural state space architectures used in modern deep generative AI systems.

Weekly Syllabus Breakdown

  • Discrete Foundations: Review of finite probability spaces, arbitrage principles, and risk-neutral measures in discrete pricing environments.
  • Measure Theory: Rigorous study of σ-algebras, Lebesgue integration, conditional expectations, and discrete martingale inequalities.
  • Brownian Motion: Construction, path properties, quadratic variation, and the mathematical properties of continuous limits.
  • Itô Calculus: Continuous-time stochastic integration, Itô’s Lemma derivation, and multi-dimensional diffusion frameworks.
  • SDEs & Change of Measure: Stochastic differential equations, Girsanov’s Theorem, risk-neutral measures, and the Radon-Nikodym derivative.
  • Term Structure Modeling: Analytical research into Vasicek, Cox-Ingersoll-Ross (CIR), and short-rate models for fixed income derivatives.
